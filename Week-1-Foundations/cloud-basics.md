Cloud computing is the on demand provision or access of IT resources on a pay as you basis, over the internet.
Iaas also Infrastructure as service is a cloud model where the the cloud provider provides and maintains only the underlinig infrastructure for IT resources, all other services include maintainence of software is done by the client. Paas ..Platform as a service is where the cloud provider takes up some more responsbilities of providing the building sofware blocks with which the clients create software products. Saas also software as a service is where the provider not only provides the sofware resources but also maintains both the underlining physical and sofware infrastructure for clients.Clients only need use the software for their various business needs.
Cloud as mentioned earlier is the on demand availability of IT resources over the internet. Businesses use the resources as and when their needs scale up or down. With on premises, business own the physical servers themselves, which they pay upfront for. These servers could be under utilised there is less demand for IT resources and could be overwhelmed when there is an excess demand for IT resources.
Capex ,capital expenditure involves fixed assets that a business acquire. eg servers. This is linked to on premises. Opex on the other hand are costs incurred in the cost of the operation of the business and its linked to the adoption of cloud.
